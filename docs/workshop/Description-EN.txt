[h1]Individual languages for every pawn[/h1]
Rim Languages — Individual Learning is an addon for Cybranian — Rim Languages, for RimWorld 1.6.

Each pawn has their own language and dialect knowledge. Learning a language improves that pawn's personal proficiency rather than automatically sharing their knowledge with the entire faction.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]A Languages tab for each pawn, showing known languages, dialects, proficiency and native identity.
[*]Individual learning from Cybranian's books and lessons with prisoners.
[*]Automatic transmission during ordinary conversations. Pawns can teach knowledge mastered at 100%, without manual orders or priority over other social interactions.
[*]Progress influenced by social abilities. Uses Cybranian's difficulty setting, without a second speed slider.
[*]Recruitment, resistance, conversion, trade and communications use the acting pawn's knowledge. Social factors account for shared mastered languages and dialects.
[*]Knowledge persists through saving, recruitment and caravans.
[*]Developer tools to add a language or dialect to one pawn and precisely adjust proficiency.
[/list]

[h2]Optional DLC features[/h2]
[b]Biotech:[/b] supported newborns start their maternal language and dialect at 0%, then learn through feeding and play. Vanilla education, skills, growth and passions remain independent of language progress.

[b]Ideology:[/b] learning events and thoughts follow personal progress when a Cybranian language-learning precept is present.

[b]No DLC is required.[/b]

[h2]What is preserved from Cybranian[/h2]
Reuses the parent's languages, dialects, books, initial configuration and difficulty setting. Faction rows display language identity without collective proficiency; collective conversation XP messages are hidden.

Collective data remains active internally for compatibility and fallbacks when individual data cannot be used.

[h2]Existing saves[/h2]
Can be added to an existing Cybranian save. Pawns without individual records receive only their observed faction's native language and dialect; other faction knowledge is not imported. The historical origin of pawns recruited before installation cannot be reconstructed. Keep a backup of your save before adding the addon.

Mods replacing social interactions, births or infant care may require adaptation; compatibility is not guaranteed.
